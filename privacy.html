Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This is the personal professional website of Eduardo Ferrari. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information the site handles and why. It covers the home page (in English, Portuguese, Spanish and Japanese), the Insights page, and this site's Privacy and Terms pages. Other pages hosted under the same domain, such as invitation-only pages, are separate and are not covered by this policy.

Information you choose to send

The contact form on the home page asks for your name, your email address, and a message. When you submit it, that information is sent to Formspree, a form-handling service, which delivers it to me by email. I use it only to read and respond to your message. I do not sell it, and I do not add you to any mailing list.

To filter out automated spam, the home page uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile runs when the home page loads and evaluates signals from your browser to tell people apart from bots.

Analytics — only if you allow it

The site can use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are viewed. It is off by default. Until you choose to allow it, the Google Analytics script is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

If you allow analytics, Google collects usage information such as pages viewed, referring site, device and browser type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address, and sets cookies whose names begin with _ga. If you later decline, measurement stops in that browser and the site removes those cookies.

Stored in your browser

The site keeps two small preferences in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are not sent to any server by this site:

  • theme — whether you prefer the light or dark appearance.
  • analytics_consent — your analytics choice, so you are not asked on every visit.

Third-party services

Loading the site involves these providers, each under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information.
  • Formspree and Cloudflare Turnstile, for the contact form, as described above.
  • Google Analytics, only if you allow it, as described above.

Articles are linked on LinkedIn. Following those links takes you to LinkedIn, where its own policy applies.

How long information is kept

Messages you send are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation. Formspree, Google and the other providers above retain data according to their own policies and settings.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete a message you sent through the contact form. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and I will respond to reasonable requests. You can also decline analytics using the buttons above, and you can block or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

Children

This site is intended for professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, use the contact form.
