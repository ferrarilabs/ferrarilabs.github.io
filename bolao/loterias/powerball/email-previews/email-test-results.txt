Powerball email professionalization — admin test-send evidence (ROUND 2)
==========================================================================

Round 1 was REJECTED by Eduardo — 3 real bugs found (mismatched percentage
between templates, an unreconciled financial summary, and a correction email
describing a change that didn't actually exist in the ticket data). Root
cause: the three templates were not consuming one single, validated,
internally-consistent snapshot. Round 2 fixes this by introducing a single
shared fixture (scripts/email/fixtures/powerball-email-test-fixture.json)
that all three flows build from, plus hard validation gates (financial
reconciliation, cross-template consistency, real ticket-diff for
corrections) that block the send instead of allowing another bad email out.
See docs/bolao/loterias/POWERBALL_EMAIL_ARCHITECTURE.md for the full
before/after and scripts/audit_email_tests_round2.mjs for regression tests
reproducing all three original bugs (they now fail loudly instead of
sending).
