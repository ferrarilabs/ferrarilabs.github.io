About

Owner-operated. Technically hands-on.

FerrariLabs is run by Eduardo Ferrari. I started as a developer and DBA, moved into team lead roles, and have spent most of my career designing and running financial crime technology platforms — across US, Canadian, European, Middle Eastern, Asia-Pacific, and Latin American operating environments.

The same habits apply whether it is a bank's monitoring platform or a local business's booking process: find out what is actually going wrong, fix that, and keep the solution simple enough to maintain.

My financial crime technology experience is professional background and published writing, not a current service offering. See Background.

Based in Concord, North Carolina. I work in English and Portuguese, with conversational Spanish.
