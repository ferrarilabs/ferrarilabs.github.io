Services

Four ways we improve the customer path.

01

Get Found

Websites, Google Business Profile, local-search foundations, domain and business-email basics. A new focused local-business site typically starts around $1,500; smaller repairs cost less.

02

Don't Lose the Lead

Quote requests, contact forms, click-to-call, lead routing and simple follow-up so inquiries reach the right person quickly. Typical focused projects are roughly $900–$2,000, depending on the workflow.

03

Run Smarter

Scheduling, CRM connections, repetitive admin automation, practical AI assistance and small internal tools. We map the workflow first and quote the work after the problem is clear.

04

Fix What's Broken

Stale pages, broken forms, confusing calls to action, domain/email problems, unfinished templates and other trust-killing details. A tightly scoped repair is often $450–$950.

Third-party software, advertising spend and paid platform subscriptions stay separate from FerrariLabs fees. There is no automatic monthly retainer after a one-time fix.

About

Based in Concord, NC.

FerrariLabs works directly with small businesses around Concord, Charlotte and surrounding communities, as well as businesses throughout North Carolina and South Carolina — in person where that's practical, remotely where it makes more sense.

FerrariLabs is owner-operated, so you work directly with the person doing the work: me, Eduardo Ferrari. I started as a software developer and database administrator and later designed and ran complex technology platforms for financial institutions. The same discipline applies here: understand the current process, fix what is actually broken, and keep the solution maintainable.

Service is available in English and Portuguese. The company serves local businesses broadly; Portuguese is simply an additional way to work with me, not a separate brand.
